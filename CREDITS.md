# Credits and sources

Agerdin is an independent collection of Bangladeshi childhood games and memories. It combines original game code, a custom wordmark/favicon, original CSS illustrations, cited research, and third-party media.

## Photography and game art

- `dist/game-thumbnails.jpg` is a locally bundled contact sheet used by playable-game cards. Per-game image source notes appear in the site where available.
- The 99-image field-day photo gallery and 99 non-playable folk-game archive cards use the same distinct Wikimedia Commons photographs. Every archive card maps to one unique image record; its alt text describes the photograph, and its dialog exposes contributor, license, and original file page. Check each original file page before reuse.
- Other game previews may load from their original source hosts. They are not bundled in the thumbnail sheet and may change or block embedding. Their source links are displayed beside the relevant content where available.
- The Agerdin open-book-and-kite mark is an original project SVG and is shared by the browser favicon, site header, loading screen, and matching PWA icons. The Web Audio sound cues are synthesized in the browser; no audio track is bundled.

## Research and game references

Rules and cultural context are linked in the site to sources including Banglapedia, bdnews24.com, The Daily Star, Prothom Alo, TBS News, UNESCO, Sesame Workshop, and the Bangladesh Teachers Portal. Outbound game links are shown on their cards and open in a new tab.

## Fonts

- The current build self-hosts Noto Sans Bengali and Noto Serif Bengali, regular and bold. Their license notice is `dist/assets/fonts/OFL-Noto.txt`. These are temporary local substitutes for the requested Hind Siliguri and Literata families; those exact font files were not available in the build environment.
