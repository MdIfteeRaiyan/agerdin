Agerdin static site — Google Search Console verification

The Google site-verification meta tag is already in index.html inside <head>.
Deploy this folder (or its ZIP contents) to Vercel with Framework Preset: Other,
Build Command: leave blank, and Output Directory: .

After the deployment is live on the exact domain/property added in Google Search Console,
open the live homepage and verify that its page source contains:
<meta name="google-site-verification" content="2A9GBDVUBb4EHR1uPaEhCVuAMNRIhvDUQdlgH9Vo6JY">
Then choose HTML tag verification in Search Console and press Verify.
